About This Program

A student-athlete's success is rarely theirs alone. Behind every young person navigating academics, athletics, and the future is a family trying to help — often without a roadmap. The Athlete & Family Education Support program gives families the resources and confidence to guide their students through it.

From understanding NCAA eligibility rules to navigating FAFSA, from mental health conversations to life planning after sports — this program puts practical, accessible information in the hands of the people who matter most.

What We Offer

Family Workshops

Interactive sessions covering college recruiting, financial aid, academic requirements, and how to best support a student-athlete at home.

Financial Literacy & Aid Navigation

Practical guidance on FAFSA, scholarships, athletic aid, and building a financial plan for post-secondary education.

Recruiting & Eligibility Guidance

Clear explanations of NCAA/NAIA eligibility requirements, recruiting timelines, and what families can and can't do during the process.

Life Planning Resources

Tools for families to have meaningful conversations about life beyond sports — career, identity, finances, and long-term goals.

Who Can Participate

  • Parents and guardians of current or aspiring student-athletes
  • Student-athletes at any level seeking family-inclusive support
  • Families navigating the college recruiting process for the first time
  • Community members who want to better support youth athletes in their network

Support the Whole Family

Great athletes are built by great support systems. Let us help yours become one.

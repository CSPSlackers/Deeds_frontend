About This Program

The Youth Wellness & Student Success program is designed to meet students where they are — in schools, in their communities, and in the pivotal years between middle and high school when habits, mindsets, and futures take shape.

Our curriculum blends evidence-based wellness practices with life skills coaching and academic accountability structures, empowering students to show up fully — for school, for themselves, and for their communities.

What We Offer

Wellness Workshops

Interactive sessions covering mental health, stress management, nutrition, and physical well-being tailored to adolescent development.

Life Skills Coaching

Goal-setting, communication, time management, and decision-making skills taught through real-world scenarios and peer mentorship.

Academic Accountability

Progress tracking, study strategies, and consistent check-ins to keep students on track with their academic goals.

Community Integration

Connecting students to local resources, volunteer opportunities, and community leaders who reflect their potential.

Who Can Participate

  • Middle school students (Grades 6–8) enrolled in partnering school districts
  • High school students (Grades 9–12) seeking mentorship and life skills development
  • Students referred by school counselors, teachers, or parents
  • No prior participation or application experience required

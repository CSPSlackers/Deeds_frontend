Industry Voices

Career exposure and industry mentorship connecting students with professionals across sectors to expand their vision of what's possible for their future.

← Back to All Programs

About This Program

Many students can't picture themselves in a career because they've never met anyone who does that work. Industry Voices changes that by bringing professionals directly to students — through panels, site visits, and one-on-one mentorship — to expand what they believe is possible for themselves.

This pilot program is currently launching with professionals from a diverse range of sectors, intentionally curated to reflect the communities and backgrounds of the students we serve.

What We Offer

Professional Panels

Live Q&A sessions where students hear directly from professionals about their career paths, challenges, and advice they wish they'd received earlier.

Site Visits & Job Shadows

Curated experiences at partner organizations where students can see industries in action and ask real questions in real environments.

One-on-One Mentorship

Ongoing mentorship matches between students and industry professionals who share their background, interests, or career goals.

Career Exploration Tools

Guided exercises helping students discover their strengths, map them to industries, and build an early sense of professional direction.

Industries Represented

Our growing network of industry partners spans sectors including:

Healthcare Technology Sports & Athletics Law & Policy Education Business & Finance Media & Communications Nonprofit & Public Service Trades & Skilled Work

Who Can Participate

  • High school students (Grades 9–12) open to career exploration
  • Professionals in any industry interested in mentoring youth
  • Organizations looking to engage with D.A.D.'s student community
  • Schools seeking to supplement career and technical education (CTE) programming

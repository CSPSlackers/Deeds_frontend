About This Program

Most advisory programs reset every year — a new advisor, a new relationship, starting over. The Student Success Advisory Pilot is different. Advisors stay with the same students across multiple grade levels, building the kind of trust and consistency that actually changes outcomes.

This continuity model means advisors know each student's history, understand their growth trajectory, and can provide genuinely personalized guidance — not generic advice handed down from a stranger every fall.

What We Offer

Longitudinal Mentorship

Advisors follow the same students across grade levels — no re-introducing yourself, no losing context. Real relationships built over time.

Goal Tracking

Structured goal-setting sessions at the start of each term with ongoing check-ins to monitor progress and adapt strategies.

Transition Planning

Intentional planning at each academic transition point — middle to high school, high school to post-secondary — so students are never caught off guard.

Family Engagement

Parents and guardians are looped in at key milestones, ensuring home and school environments reinforce the same goals.

Who Can Participate

  • Middle school students (Grades 6–8) in the pilot cohort schools
  • Students who commit to multi-year participation for full continuity benefit
  • School partners willing to integrate advisory scheduling into the school day
  • Families who want to be actively engaged in their student's advisory journey

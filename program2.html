About This Program

Athletics teaches discipline, teamwork, and resilience — but life after sports requires a different playbook. The Athlete Education & Transition Portal bridges the gap between an athlete's identity on the field and their potential off it.

Through structured guidance, this program helps student-athletes plan for post-secondary education, explore career pathways, and build the skills needed to thrive when the final whistle blows.

What We Offer

College Planning

Guidance on college applications, athletic eligibility (NCAA/NAIA), financial aid, and choosing the right academic path alongside sports.

Career Pathway Mapping

One-on-one support exploring career options that leverage athletic strengths — leadership, discipline, and performance under pressure.

Identity Transition Support

Workshops on managing the psychological shift when athletic identity evolves, building a full sense of self beyond the sport.

Resource Portal

Curated digital resources including scholarship databases, internship pipelines, and alumni mentors who made the same transition.

Who Can Participate

  • High school student-athletes in Grades 10–12 preparing for life after sports
  • College athletes exploring professional transitions or career pivots
  • Recent graduates navigating post-athletic career planning
  • Coaches and educators seeking transition resources for their athletes

Start Your Transition Journey

Whether you're still competing or just finished your last season, we're here to help you build what comes next.
